    Looking for your Micro Plus

    Turn on the amp and close Fender Tone on your other devices. This page connects by itself once the amp is found.

    Preset editor

    Select a pedal or the amp to adjust it. You'll hear your changes on the amp as you make them, but they aren't kept until you save.

    Your pedalboard

      Keyboard: / opens the presets, [ and ] change preset, and Ctrl or ⌘ + S saves. On a knob, scroll to turn it, hold Shift while dragging for fine changes, or select its number to type a value.

      Appearance

      Save as copy

      Your current preset stays as it is, and you keep editing it afterwards.

      Groups only change how this page lists your presets. Nothing on your amp changes.
