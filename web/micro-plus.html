    Your pedalboard

      Drag a pedal to change the order your sound goes through them, or use the arrows under it. You'll hear a new order after you save.

      Keyboard: / opens the presets, [ and ] change preset, C adds a preset to compare, Space or 1 to 6 switch between the ones you're comparing, and Ctrl or ⌘ + S saves. On a knob, scroll to turn it, hold Shift while dragging for fine changes, or select its number to type a value.
